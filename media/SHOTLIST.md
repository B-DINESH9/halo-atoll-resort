## F1 — Hero Scroll Film (`media/raw/film.mp4`)
- **Type:** Vertical video (`image-to-video` from `K1` to `K2`)
- **Aspect ratio / Size / Duration:** `9:16`, `720x1280`, `15s`, one continuous unbroken shot
- **Start frame:** `media/raw/k1.png` | **End frame:** `media/raw/k2.png`
- **LOCKED:** beats in order - the marked-out site from above -> the camera dives in for one dramatic close detail of the foundations -> back up as the structure rises and closes -> pulls up to reveal the finished building. One continuous move, no cuts.
- **Prompt:** Vertical cinematic film of a circular ring of twelve timber overwater suites being built on a turquoise lagoon reef at golden hour. Opens high above looking straight down: a perfect circle of thirty timber piles staked out in clear shallow reef water. In one unbroken continuous camera move with no cuts, the camera dives gracefully down beneath the clear turquoise surface to reveal one timber pile driven deep into clean sunlit coral sand surrounded by shimmering water caustics. Sweeping back upward above the surface, pale timber beams, curved decking, and slatted timber battens rise and interlock around the circle to close the ring. The camera pulls steadily upward as the central open-sky pool court fills and settles, sun loungers and palms appear, warm golden lights glow along the ring and slender arrival jetty, revealing the finished circular retreat centred in the turquoise lagoon. Soft natural golden-hour light, photoreal architectural visualisation, no text, no logos, no cuts.
